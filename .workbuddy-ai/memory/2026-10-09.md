### Design faisle

- **Alag controller, `/Retail/` ke andar nahi.** Technician aur accounts clerk ko
  bhi wahi filters chahiye. `[Authorize(Roles = NexusRoles.StaffRoles)]`.
- **Do result sets:** orders `CreatedAt` par, lines `ActivatedOn` par. Ek period,
  do columns — SRS ka *"date or period of application or received the connection"*.
- **Khaali query = kuch nahi.** Bina filter poori book nahi dikhati.
- **`To` poora din cover karta hai** — `To.Date.AddDays(1)` se `<` compare. Warna
  31 Oct ka din chhoot jata. **Ye date-range ka standard trap hai.**
- **GET form** — URL shareable.

### Gotcha — record ke field naam

`SearchOrderRow` ke fields `ServiceTypeName` / `PlanName` / `StatusLabel` hain,
`ConnectionType` / `Plan` / `Status` **nahi**. Positional record hai, to service
mein order matter karta hai magar view mein naam se access hota hai — pehle view
mein ghalat naam likh diya tha.
